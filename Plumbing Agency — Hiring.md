# Plumbing Agency — Hiring

> **Parent:** [[Plumbing Agency — Game Plan]]
> **Principle:** Hire for leverage, not for company. Every hire should free you to do higher-value work.

---

## Hiring Sequence

```
Month 0-2:  YOU (everything)
Month 3:    VA (Philippines/LATAM) — fulfillment + admin
Month 6:    SDR #1 — outbound sales (once you've proven the model)
Month 9:    Tech support / junior dev — pipeline maintenance, integrations
Month 12:   Account manager — client success for Tier 2/3 clients
Month 18:   SDR #2 — second geo expansion
```

---

## Role #1: Virtual Assistant — Month 3

**Trigger:** You have 3+ clients and spend >10 hrs/week on non-sales work.

### What They Do

| Responsibility | Hours/Week | Details |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| Client onboarding support | 5 | Follow onboarding checklist, configure basic Twilio settings, test pipelines |
| Weekly report generation | 3 | Pull dashboard data, format email, send to client (you approve first month) |
| Pipeline monitoring | 5 | Check webhook health daily, verify text-backs delivering, flag issues |
| Billing & invoicing | 2 | Send invoices via Stripe, follow up on late payments |
| Apollo list building | 5 | Generate fresh lead lists, enrich data, load into outreach sequence |
| Admin / misc | 5 | Contract prep, scheduling, research tasks |
| **Total** | **25** | |

### Hiring Spec

- **Location:** Philippines, LATAM, or Eastern Europe (timezone overlap with US)
- **English:** Fluent written, conversational spoken
- **Tech:** Comfortable with SaaS tools (Notion, Stripe, Apollo, Google Sheets). No coding required.
- **Rate:** $6-10/hr (Philippines) / $8-15/hr (LATAM)
- **Monthly cost:** $1,000-$2,000
- **Where to find:** OnlineJobs.ph, Upwork, Athena, BruntWork

### Interview Process

1. **Written application** — "Tell me about a time you learned a new software tool to solve a problem."
2. **Paid test task** — Give them a real Apollo list build. Time them. Grade accuracy. ($50 for 2 hours)
3. **Video interview** — Communication clarity, reliability signals, availability
4. **Trial week** — Part-time, paid. Handle real client reports with your oversight.

## Role #2: SDR (Sales Development Rep) — Month 6

**Trigger:** You've closed 5+ clients, your outreach process is documented, and you're the bottleneck on sales volume.

### What They Do

| Responsibility | Hours/Week | Details |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| Cold calls | 15 | 100-150 calls/week using your script |
| Email/SMS sequences | 5 | Load leads into sequences, monitor replies |
| Demo booking | 5 | Schedule qualified demos on your calendar |
| CRM hygiene | 3 | Update lead status, notes, follow-ups |
| List building | 2 | Refine Apollo searches, test new segments |
| **Total** | **30** | |

### Hiring Spec

- **Location:** US-based (must sound native on calls)
- **Experience:** 1+ years outbound B2B sales (home services a plus but not required)
- **Key trait:** Resilient. Volume-driven. Can handle rejection.
- **Comp:** $40-50K base + $100/demo-attended + $250/client-closed commission
- **Monthly cost:** $4,000-5,500 (base) + $1,500-3,000 (commission)
- **Where to find:** LinkedIn, Indeed, Bravado, commission-only sales groups

### Ramp Plan

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 1 | Shadow your calls, learn the script, study the product |
| 2 | Make calls with you listening, live coaching |
| 3 | Independent calls, daily debrief, conversion tracking |
| 4 | Full quota: 100-150 calls/week, target 5-8 demos booked |

**Kill switch:** If they can't book 3 demos/week by Week 4, replace them. Sales is measurable. Don't wait.

## Role #3: Tech Support / Junior Dev — Month 9

**Trigger:** Pipeline issues interrupt your sales time, or client count exceeds what you can support solo.

### What They Do

- Monitor pipeline health, respond to alerts
- Debug webhook failures, Twilio config issues
- Build new CRM integrations (ServiceTitan → Housecall Pro → Jobber)
- Maintain and improve the dashboard
- Handle client technical onboarding
- Tune LLM classifiers for new client verticals

### Hiring Spec

- **Location:** Anywhere (remote)
- **Stack:** Python, FastAPI, Twilio, basic DevOps (Railway/VPS, health checks)
- **Experience:** 2-4 years. Full-stack capable, API integration experience.
- **Comp:** $60-80K (offshore) / $100-120K (US)
- **Monthly cost:** $5,000-$10,000
- **Where to find:** Wellfound (AngelList), YC Work at a Startup, LinkedIn, Toptal

---

## Role #4: Account Manager — Month 12

**Trigger:** 15+ clients, you're doing 15 hours/month of strategy calls and can't grow.

### What They Do

- Monthly strategy calls (replaces you)
- Quarterly business reviews
- Upsell Tier 2 → Tier 3
- Renewal management
- Churn risk detection and intervention
- Client feedback → product roadmap

### Hiring Spec

- **Location:** US-based (client-facing)
- **Experience:** 2+ years customer success or account management (SaaS or agency)
- **Comp:** $60-70K base + retention bonus + upsell commission
- **Monthly cost:** $5,000-7,000

---

## Compensation Philosophy

| Principle | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Base covers bills, commission drives behavior.** | SDRs get base + per-demo + per-close. They control their income. |
| **Retention bonus for account managers.** | Quarterly bonus tied to client retention rate. Aligns incentives. |
| **Contractors before employees.** | VA, junior dev start as contractors. Convert to employee at 6+ months if great. |
| **Profit share at 2+ years.** | Key people get 1-3% profit share. Makes leaving expensive. |

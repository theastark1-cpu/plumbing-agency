## The Core Offer

**"We answer every phone call your plumbing company gets — including nights, weekends, and holidays — and book the jobs you're currently losing to voicemail."**

Not "we build AI chatbots" or "we run Google Ads." The offer IS the missed-call-to-booked-job pipeline, proven with real attribution.

## Retainer Justification (The Sales Script)

When a plumbing owner pushes back on price, the math weapon:

1. **"How many calls do you miss to voicemail each month?"** (They'll guess 20-40)
2. **"What's your average job ticket?"** (Usually $400-$800 service, $3K-$15K install)
3. **"If we capture just 3-4 of those missed calls... at $800 avg... that's $2,400-$3,200 in new revenue. Our retainer is $2,500. You're cash-flow positive on month one."**

The retainer disappears into the revenue it generates. That's the fund-grade LTV/CAC modeling from the rubric applied directly to their business.

## What We Don't Do

| Not our game | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Google Ads / PPC management | Commodity. Every agency does this. Zero moat. |
| SEO / content marketing | Long-tail, slow, doesn't leverage the AI edge. |
| Social media management | Plumbing owners don't care about Instagram. They care about phones ringing. |
| Website builds | One-and-done project work. Not recurring. Not our model. |

Refer these out for a finder's fee. Stay focused on the recurring-revenue pipeline.

## The Unfair Bundle

If you land a plumbing client AND they have an HVAC division (many do), the same pipeline serves both. Cross-sell instantly — half the onboarding work for full additional retainer. This is the multi-location play that makes Tier 3 compound.

---

## Pricing Psychology

- **Tier 1 is the foot in the door.** Cheap enough to try, immediately ROI-positive.
- **Tier 2 is where you want them.** Full value delivery, strong retention.
- **Tier 3 is aspirational.** For the ones who want to fire their front-office software. Fewer clients, higher LTV, lower churn.

**Upgrade path:** Start everyone on Tier 1 or 2. Within 90 days, the results data sells the upgrade for you.

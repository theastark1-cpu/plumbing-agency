## Three Reactivation Pools

### 1. Dormant Past Customers

| Segment | Timeframe | Reactivation Rate | Value Per Recovery |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Warm dormant | 12-18 months | 12-18% | $400-$1,200 |
| Cool dormant | 18-36 months | 6-12% | $300-$900 |
| Cold dormant | 36+ months | 2-5% | $200-$600 |

### 2. Unconverted Leads & Quotes

| Type | Recovery Strategy | Expected Recovery |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Quote sent, no response | 48hr check-in → 7-day value → 14-day offer | 10-15% |
| Called, didn't book | "Still need help?" SMS at 3 and 7 days | 8-12% |
| Web form, never followed up | Auto-reply → 24hr SMS → 72hr call | 15-25% |
| No-show appointment | Same-day reschedule → 3-day check-in | 20-30% |

### 3. Maintenance Plan Lapses

- Lapse rate after Year 1: 25-40%
- 90-day grace period recovery: 40-55%
- Value of recovered plan customer: $1,200-$2,400/year

## Tier Integration

### Tier 1 — Reactivation Additions

- CRM dormant scan (flag all 12+ month inactive)
- Automated SMS check-in (3-touch over 14 days)
- Unconverted lead rescue (48hr → 7-day → 14-day)
- Monthly recovery report

### Tier 2 — Reactivation Additions

- Smart segmentation (Warm/Cool/Cold + job-type cohorts)
- Multi-channel cascade (SMS → email → AI voice)
- Seasonal trigger campaigns
- Maintenance plan win-back
- No-show recovery automation
- Attribution dashboard (recovered vs. new vs. captured revenue)

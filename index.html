Plumbing's Edge Over HVAC

Plumbing has one structural advantage HVAC doesn't: emergency urgency premium. A burst pipe or sewage backup is a 911-level household crisis — higher stakes, higher willingness-to-pay, and higher emotional relief when solved. Missed-call capture is more valuable (every missed emergency call = lost $500-$5K). Emergency calls are sticky — if you save someone at 2 AM, they're yours for life.

Core Thesis

Voice-transcription + missed-call-text-back pipeline that captures emergency/after-hours plumbing demand competitors lose to voicemail — converting leaked calls into booked, attributed jobs, then layering maintenance-plan recurring on top of the emergency relationship.

The plumbing industry runs on phone calls. 30-40% of those calls — especially after-hours and weekend emergency calls — go to voicemail. Every missed call is a $500-$5,000 job walking to the next plumber who did answer.

Your agency doesn't compete on ads. It competes on capture rate: answer every call, transcribe it, text the lead back within 60 seconds, book the job, and track the revenue. Then layer maintenance plans on top to convert one-time emergency customers into recurring revenue relationships.

Incumbents (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber) sell software subscriptions. You're selling a system that replaces their front office for lead capture, triage, booking, and follow-up — with attribution they've never had.

Speed-to-lead on positive replies is the #1 predictor of close rate. Set up Slack notifications for any reply. If someone replies with intent and you wait 4 hours, close rate drops 60%.

The Live Demo (Phone, Warm from Email)

When a prospect replies positively, your next move is a phone call — but they're already warm from the email sequence. That's the entire point.

"Hey [First Name] — [Your Name]. Thanks for replying.
Wanted to walk you through this live, takes 5 minutes.

Pull up your phone — I'm going to have you call this number:
[Your Twilio Number]. Just let it ring to voicemail and say
whatever you'd say to a real plumbing emergency customer.
Go ahead, I'll wait."

[They call, leave voicemail]

"Perfect — watch your phone. You should see a text in about
60 seconds."

[Text arrives]

"That's what happens to every missed call your shop gets.
The ones going to voicemail right now. How many do you think
that is per month?"

Why the phone is the close, not the opener: Calls from cold prospects get 5-8% pickup. Calls from email-replied prospects get 60%+ pickup and convert 3-4x better.

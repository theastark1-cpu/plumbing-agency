## The Core Thesis

30-40% of plumbing calls go to voicemail. Every missed call is a $500-$5,000 job walking to the next plumber. On top of that, 30-50% of past customers are dormant and 60% of qualified leads never get followed up.

This agency doesn't compete on ads — it competes on **capture rate**: answer every call, transcribe it, text the lead back within 60 seconds, book the job, and track the revenue. Then layer dormant reactivation and lead rescue on top to turn the database into a revenue engine.

# Plumbing Agency — Sales & Acquisition

> **Parent:** [[Plumbing Agency — Game Plan]]
> **Principle:** Cold email is the door-opener. Calls only for closing. Less volume, more precision.

---

## The Motion

Cold email is the primary outbound channel. Phone calls are reserved for:
- Closing warm demos (after positive email reply)
- Following up on high-intent signals (link clicks, multiple replies)
- The "speed-to-lead" call when someone replies within 5 minutes

This isn't a soft preference. The data is clear:
- **Average cold email reply rate:** 3.43% (Instantly 2026)
- **Top 10% of senders:** 10%+ reply rate
- **First email captures 58% of replies** — the rest come from follow-ups
- **Best emails are <80 words**, single CTA, problem-first
- **Peak engagement:** Tuesday-Wednesday
- **Cold call connect rate:** 5-8% (and falling). Cold call "kill rate" from owners: 90%+ go to voicemail.

Cold email wins on volume capacity, reply quality, and the fact that **plumbing owners check email between jobs but won't pick up an unknown number.**

## Target Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Company size | 3-25 trucks (too small = can't afford; too big = PE-owned, gatekeepers) |
| Owner involvement | Owner-operator, not absentee. Makes marketing/front-office decisions. |
| Current pain | "I lose calls to voicemail on nights/weekends" or "my dispatcher can't keep up" |
| Tech stack | Already uses ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber (proves tech willingness) |
| Geo | Single metro area. Don't scatter — density creates word-of-mouth. |
| Annual revenue | $500K-$5M |

**Anti-targets:**
- Franchise locations (decision-maker is corporate, not local)
- PE-owned rollups (purchasing department, not owner)
- Solo operators with 1 truck (can't afford $1,500+ retainer)
- Owners who "don't believe in technology" (don't waste your time)

## Cold Email Infrastructure (Week 0 — Build Before You Send)

### Domain Setup (Critical — Skip This and You Go to Spam)

You need **3-5 sending domains** that look like real companies, not your main one. If your main domain gets burned, your business email is gone.

| Component | Setup |
|-----------|-------|
| Domains | Buy 3-5 variations of your brand (e.g., youragency.com, youragency.io, youragency.co, getyouragency.com, tryyouragency.com) |
| Cost | $10-12/year per domain (Namecheap, Cloudflare Registrar) |
| Email accounts | 2-3 inboxes per domain (Google Workspace $7/mo per inbox, or Zoho Mail $1/mo) |
| Authentication | SPF + DKIM + DMARC records — non-negotiable |
| Warmup | Use Instantly.ai ($30/mo) or Warmbox.ai to auto-warm inboxes for 2-4 weeks before sending |
| Sending tool | Instantly.ai (best for high-volume) or Smartlead (cheaper, similar features) |

**Total infrastructure cost: ~$80-150/mo for 5-10 sending inboxes.**

### Warmup Schedule

| Week | Emails/Day/Inbox | Notes |
|------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | 5 | Auto-warmup via Instantly. Don't send manual emails yet. |
| 2 | 10 | Continue warmup. Test 5 manual emails to yourself first. |
| 3 | 20 | Start small manual campaigns. Monitor bounce rate. |
| 4+ | 30-50 | Scale. Never exceed 50/day/inbox — Google's threshold. |

### Volume Math

- 5 domains × 3 inboxes each = 15 inboxes
- 30 emails/day/inbox = **450 emails/day**
- 5 days/week = **2,250 emails/week**
- At 3.43% reply rate = **77 replies/week**
- 30% positive intent = **23 qualified conversations/week**
- 25% close rate = **6 new clients/week**

**That's the target scale by Month 3.** Don't start there. Start at 50/day, scale as deliverability holds.

## Cold Email Sequence (7 Touches, 18 Days)

### Why 7 Touches?

- Under 4: giving up too early (58% of replies come from email 1, but 42% come from follow-ups)
- Over 7: diminishing returns unless each adds new value
- 7 is the sweet spot for top-quartile senders

### Sequence Overview

| Step | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|------|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Day 1 | Email | **The Pattern Interrupt** — open with problem, not solution |
| 2 | Day 4 | Email | **Case study / social proof** — show, don't tell |
| 3 | Day 7 | Email | **The Direct Offer** — clear ask, no pressure |
| 4 | Day 10 | Email | **Value-Add** — send something useful (resource, data, tool) |
| 5 | Day 13 | Email | **Reactivation angle** — dormant customer pitch |
| 6 | Day 16 | Email | **Breakup email** — last touch, respectful close |
| 7 | Day 18 | LinkedIn | Connection request (if no email reply) |

### Email #1 — "The Pattern Interrupt" (Day 1)

**Subject lines (A/B test 2-3):**
- `[First Name], quick question about [Company]'s calls`
- `how many calls [Company] misses on weekends`
- `the 2 AM call problem`

**Body (<80 words):**

```
Hey [First Name],

Saw [Company] is one of the bigger plumbing outfits in [Metro].
Quick question — do you have any idea how many of your calls
go to voicemail on nights and weekends?

Most 10-15 truck shops I work with are losing 30-40% of their
emergency calls to voicemail. At $800 avg ticket, that's
$15-25K/month walking to the next plumber who answered.

I built a system that catches every missed call, texts the
customer back in 60 seconds, and books the job — before you
even see the voicemail.

Worth a quick look?

[Your Name]
[Calendar Link]
```

**Why this works:** Problem-first, specific dollar number, single CTA (binary), <80 words, references their company by name.

## Reply Handling Playbook

When someone replies, your response time is the conversion rate.

| Reply Type | Response Time | Next Action |
|------------|--------------|-------------|
| Positive ("Tell me more") | **<5 minutes** | Send calendar link + 1-line context |
| Question ("How does it work?") | **<30 minutes** | Send short answer + Loom demo (2 min) |
| Objection ("Too expensive") | **<2 hours** | Send ROI math specific to their business |
| "Not now" / "Bad timing" | **<24 hours** | Mark as nurture, re-engage in 60 days |
| Wrong person ("Talk to my GM") | **<24 hours** | Ask for intro or pivot to GM |

**Critical rule:** Speed-to-lead on positive replies is the #1 predictor of close rate. Set up Instantly.ai auto-replies or use a Slack notification for any reply.

## Objection Handling (Email-First)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have an answering service" | "Does it text the customer back in 60 seconds? Book into your CRM automatically? Tell you which calls turned into revenue? If not, you're still leaking." |
| "It's too expensive" | "Let's do the math. How many calls do you miss per month? At your average ticket of [$X], if we capture just [Y] of those, that's [$Z] in revenue. Our retainer is less than half of that. You're cash-flow positive in month one." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Let me run a free 14-day audit on your line. No commitment. You'll see exactly how many calls you're losing and what they're worth. Then you decide with real data." |
| "We use ServiceTitan, it already does this" | "ServiceTitan is great for dispatching. But it doesn't answer the phone at 2 AM, transcribe the voicemail, text the customer back, and book the job — all before you wake up. We sit on top of ServiceTitan, not instead of it." |
| "My wife/dispatcher handles calls" | "And when they're asleep? Or on another call? Or it's Sunday? Every missed call is a job your competitor got. We're the safety net, not the replacement." |

## The Live Demo (Phone Triggered from Email)

When a prospect replies positively, your next move is a phone call — but **they're already warm from the email sequence.** That's the entire point.

```
"Hey [First Name] — [Your Name]. Thanks for replying.
Wanted to walk you through this live, takes 5 minutes.

Pull up your phone — I'm going to have you call this number:
[Your Twilio Number]. Just let it ring to voicemail and say
whatever you'd say to a real plumbing emergency customer.
Go ahead, I'll wait."

[They call, leave voicemail]

"Perfect — watch your phone. You should see a text in about
60 seconds."

[Text arrives]

"That's what happens to every missed call your shop gets.
The ones going to voicemail right now. How many do you think
that is per month?"
```

**Why the phone is the close, not the opener:** Calls from cold prospects get 5-8% pickup rates and 90%+ go to voicemail. Calls from email-replied prospects get 60%+ pickup and convert 3-4x better. The email does the work of getting past the gatekeeper; the phone does the work of getting to a yes.

## First 5 Clients — Manual Override

For the first 5 clients, add hyper-personalization that doesn't scale:

1. **Reference their Google reviews by name.** "Saw your 5-star review from Sarah M. about the water heater replacement — clearly you do great work."
2. **Reference their website copy.** If they say "24/7 emergency service," call it out: "Saw you advertise 24/7 service — wondering if you actually capture 24/7 calls."
3. **Use their specific service area.** Don't say "your metro" — say "the Oak Cliff / Lake Highlands area."
4. **Send a Loom,** not a deck. 2-minute video: "I checked out your shop, here's what I noticed about your current setup."
5. **Custom subject lines.** Skip the templates for first 5 clients. Reference something specific they did or posted.

These 5 clients become your case studies. Then the templated sequences convert better because prospects see you've done it before.

## Deliverability Discipline

This is where most cold email operations die. Follow these rules:

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Never send from your main domain** | If you get burned, your business email is gone |
| **Warm inboxes for 2-4 weeks before sending** | Cold inboxes get flagged instantly |
| **SPF + DKIM + DMARC set correctly** | Google/MS require all three |
| **Keep bounce rate <2%** | Above 2% = Google starts throttling |
| **Don't send more than 50/day/inbox** | Hard ceiling. Exceed it = spam folder. |
| **Vary copy** | Same email 1,000 times = spam trigger |
| **Clean list before sending** | Verify emails. Remove role addresses (info@, office@) |
| **Monitor placement, not opens** | Use GlockApps or Mail Tester to check inbox placement weekly |

**If deliverability drops:** Stop sending for 48 hours, reduce volume by 30%, re-warm. Don't push through — you burn the domain for weeks.

## Geo Expansion

Once you own one metro (10+ clients, replies trending positive):

1. **Pick the next geo** using the same Apollo criteria
2. **Create new sending domains** (youragency-ny.com, youragency-chi.com — or new brand variation)
3. **Run parallel sequences** with geo-specific case studies
4. **Reference clients in Geo 1** — "We work with [X] plumbing companies in [City 1], capturing [Y] calls/month."
5. **Repeat.</br>**

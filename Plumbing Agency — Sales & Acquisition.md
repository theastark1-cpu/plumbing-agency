## Target Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Company size | 3-25 trucks (too small = can't afford; too big = PE-owned, gatekeepers) |
| Owner involvement | Owner-operator, not absentee. Makes marketing/front-office decisions. |
| Current pain | "I lose calls to voicemail on nights/weekends" or "my dispatcher can't keep up" |
| Tech stack | Already uses ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber (proves tech willingness) |
| Geo | Single metro area. Don't scatter — density creates word-of-mouth. |
| Annual revenue | $500K-$5M |

**Anti-targets:**
- Franchise locations (decision-maker is corporate, not local)
- PE-owned rollups (purchasing department, not owner)
- Solo operators with 1 truck (can't afford $1,500+ retainer)
- Owners who "don't believe in technology" (don't waste your time)

## Outreach Playbook

### Sequence: "The Missed Call Audit" (5 touches over 14 days)

| Day | Channel | Message |
|-----|---------|---------|
| 1 | Cold call | "Hey [Name], quick question — do you know how many calls your shop misses to voicemail each month?" |
| 1 | Voicemail (if no answer) | Leave the URL to a Loom (2 min) showing your pipeline in action |
| 3 | Email #1 | Subject: "38% of your calls go to voicemail — here's what that costs" |
| 5 | SMS | "Hey [Name], sent you an email about missed calls. Worth 3 minutes?" |
| 7 | Email #2 | Case study: "How [Similar Plumbing Co] captured $14K in missed jobs last month" |
| 14 | Breakup email | "Haven't heard back — if call capture isn't a priority right now, I'll stop reaching out. If it is, here's my calendar." |

### Cold Call Script (60 seconds)

```
"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name]. I know you weren't expecting my call — 
can I have 30 seconds?

I work with plumbing companies on one thing: capturing the calls they're 
losing to voicemail. Most shops I talk to miss 30-40% of their after-hours 
and weekend calls. Those are emergency jobs — burst pipes, sewage backups — 
walking to the next guy who answers.

I don't sell ads. I built a system that answers every call, transcribes the 
voicemail, texts the customer back in under 60 seconds, and books the job.

Would you be open to seeing it in action? Takes 5 minutes."
```

**Key:** Don't pitch the agency. Pitch the outcome. The demo sells the product.

### Second Conversation — The Reactivation Angle

Once they've seen the demo, hit them with the database play:

```
"Let me ask you something else — how many past customers are in your database?
2,000? 3,000? 

When's the last time you reached out to the ones from 2-3 years ago?

Here's what we see across plumbing companies: 30-50% of your past customers
are dormant — haven't heard from you in 12+ months. That's 600-1,500 people
who already paid you, already trust your work.

Our system scans your entire database, finds everyone who's gone quiet,
and automatically reaches out — SMS, email, voice. Seasonal check-ins.
Maintenance reminders. 'Hey, it's been a while, everything running okay?'

Even at a conservative 10% reactivation rate at $600 average ticket,
that's $36,000-$90,000 in recovered revenue. From people already in your phone.

And the leads you're paying Google $50-150 each for? 60% of those never
get followed up. We automate that too. Every quote. Every web form.
Every no-show.

You're spending thousands on new leads while ignoring the gold mine
in your own database. We make every dollar you already spent
pay you back a second time."
```

## The Demo (5 Minutes)

This is your unfair advantage. Do NOT send a deck. Do NOT do a screen share of slides.

### Demo Flow

1. **"Call this number: [Your Twilio Number]"** — Have them call it live. Put them on speaker.
2. **Let it go to voicemail.** They leave a fake plumbing emergency message.
3. **Within 60 seconds** — their phone buzzes with a text: *"Hi, we received your call about [what they said]. Can we send a plumber? Reply YES to get scheduled."*
4. **Show the dashboard** — their call appears in the log, transcribed, classified, attributed.
5. **"That's what happens to every missed call your shop gets. The ones going to voicemail right now. How many do you think that is per month?"**

Let them do the mental math. You've just proven the value in real time.

### Why This Works

- **Zero abstraction.** They just experienced the product, not a description of it.
- **Emotional hook.** They just watched a "lost" call get captured. Now they're thinking about their OWN lost calls.
- **Instant credibility.** You're not promising. You're demonstrating.
- **No competitor can match this.** Every other agency sends a PDF deck. You make their phone buzz.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have an answering service" | "Does it text the customer back in 60 seconds? Does it book into your CRM automatically? Does it tell you which calls turned into revenue and which didn't? If not, you're still leaking." |
| "It's too expensive" | "Let's do the math. How many calls do you miss per month? [Wait for answer] At your average ticket of [$X], if we capture just [Y] of those, that's [$Z] in revenue. Our retainer is less than half of that. You're cash-flow positive in month one." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. While you think, can I set up the missed-call capture on a trial basis? No commitment. I'll route a test number, we run it for 2 weeks, and you see how many calls you're actually losing. No cost. If the data surprises you, we talk." |
| "We use ServiceTitan, it already does this" | "ServiceTitan is great for dispatching. But it doesn't answer the phone at 2 AM, transcribe the voicemail, text the customer back, and book the job — all before you wake up. We sit on top of ServiceTitan, not instead of it." |
| "My wife/dispatcher handles calls" | "And when they're asleep? Or on another call? Or it's Sunday? Every missed call is a job your competitor got. We're the safety net, not the replacement." |

## First 5 Clients — Manual Override

For the first 5 clients, do things that don't scale:

1. **Offer a 14-day free trial** — route a test number, run it live, show them the data. Hard to say no to "it's already working, here are the 12 calls we captured this week."
2. **Do the onboarding yourself.** Sit in their office for a day. Learn their dispatch flow. Configure everything.
3. **Overdeliver on reporting.** Week 1: "Here are the 8 emergency calls we captured. Here's what they would have cost you." Week 2: "Here's the trend."
4. **Ask for referrals.** After 30 days of proven results: "Is there another plumbing company owner you respect who's losing calls the same way?"

---

## Geo Expansion

Once you own one metro (10+ clients, word-of-mouth referrals organic):

1. **Pick the next geo** using the same Apollo criteria
2. **Hire a local SDR** in that geo (knows the market, owns the outreach)
3. **Reference clients in Geo 1** — "We're handling [X] plumbing companies in [City 1], capturing [Y] calls/month. Same model works here."
4. **Repeat.**
